# EXEMPLARS: short passages that show the rhythm

Examples from a different paper (a gas-solubility model, in fresh water and brine). Copy the rhythm, not the content.

These are short passages from the author's draft in this register. They are about gas solubility in water and brine. Use them to
feel sentence length, section size, the score sentence, the caption pattern and the closing list. Do not reuse any
fact, name or number.

Abstract (7 sentences, counts and two ranges, no model claims beyond them):

> Underground storage of CO2 and H2 and the production of natural gas depend on how much gas dissolves in formation
> water. This work extends the model of Ziabakhsh-Ganji and Kooi (2012) into one structure for CO2, CH4, N2, H2, H2S,
> C2H6, C3H8 and n-C4H10 in fresh water and NaCl brine. A Peng-Robinson gas phase and a Henry-law aqueous phase serve
> every gas, and only the fitted coefficients change from gas to gas. The freshwater coefficients are fitted to 5184
> data points from 251 sources. The NaCl terms are fitted to 3556 data points from 73 sources. Every gas is also
> scored by cross-validation, in which each measurement series of a source is predicted by a fit that leaves it out.

Results leaf with a single object, a figure pointer hung on a number, a mechanism sentence and a limit stated about the
model (45 to 100 words):

> The model reproduces the H2 calibration data with an AAD of 5.80 % (Figure 2). The cross-validation AAD is 7.27 %.
> The deviation depends on pressure. Below 1 MPa, it is 3.93 % (Figure S1 of the Supporting Information). That
> pressure range holds 152 of the 328 H2 data points.
>
> The model under-predicts H2 in cold water at high pressure. On the 273.15 K isotherm, the deviation reaches -17.8 %
> at the highest pressure. There, the Peng-Robinson fugacity coefficient of H2 falls below that of the reference
> equation of state. The error therefore sits in the gas phase of the cubic equation, not in the Henry constant. Cold
> water at high pressure is where the H2 model is least reliable.

A section that opens on its table and closes on a number (overview of results):

> Table 4 gives the deviations. The calibration deviation runs from 3.56 % for N2 to 11.00 % for n-C4H10 (Figures 2, 3
> and 4). In cross-validation, it runs from 3.92 % for N2 to 9.70 % for n-C4H10.

A per-object leaf (two paragraphs, three or four sentences each, numbers at the end of each):

> For CH4, the calibration AAD is 7.44 %, and the cross-validation AAD is 8.68 % (Figure 3). Its signed bias is 0.65 %.
> Its data reach 100 MPa and 473 K.
>
> The model lies below the one cold, high-pressure CH4 data set (Ou et al., 2015). These data were measured inside the
> methane hydrate region, on a metastable liquid. At 298.15 K, they also lie above the other data.
